## Recommended Demonstration Flow

### 1. Baseline

Select Bharati and run the baseline simulation.

Show:

- Fuel level
- Water and food
- Zone temperatures
- Generator status
- Forecast margin

### 2. Energy System

Open the Energy tab.

Show:

- Generator generation
- Renewable generation
- Battery SOC
- Electrical demand
- Heating demand
- Waste heat

### 3. Digital Twin Spatial View

Open Station Plan.

Show:

- Station zones
- Heating demand
- Thermal status
- Generator status

### 4. Environmental Disturbance

Apply a negative temperature offset and re-run the simulation.

Demonstrate:

Temperature decreases
→ heating demand increases
→ energy demand increases
→ generator loading increases
→ fuel consumption increases
→ projected exhaustion changes
→ resupply risk changes

### 5. Scenario Analysis

Open Scenarios and demonstrate one adverse scenario such as:

- Cold Snap
- Prolonged Blizzard
- Delayed Resupply
- Generator Failure

### 6. Alerts

Open Alerts.

Show:

- Severity
- Cause
- Evidence
- Consequence
- Recommended action

### 7. Audit and Provenance

Open Provenance & Audit.

Show:

- Parameter sources
- Assumptions
- Audit events
- Role permissions
